Sending needs Chrome, Edge or Opera on a computer. You can still download the file and copy it over by hand.

Copy it over by hand
  1. Switch off your Eurorack case. Melon must not get power from the case and from USB at the same time.
  2. Hold the small B button on the XIAO and connect it to your computer by USB.
  3. A drive called RP2350 appears. Drag the downloaded .uf2 onto it.
  4. Melon restarts by itself when the copy is done.

Radio is based on HAGIWO's MOD2 Radio Sampler. This tool runs entirely in your browser.

Switch off your case first

Melon must not get power from your Eurorack case while it is connected by USB. Switch the case off, then connect Melon to this computer with a USB-C cable.

Restart Melon for the update

Your browser asks which device to use. Pick the one called XIAO RP2350.

Melon is not in the list

Unplug the USB cable, hold the small B button on the XIAO and plug it back in. A drive called RP2350 appears.

Choose the RP2350 drive

A drive called RP2350 has appeared. Choose it in the next window, then let the site save to it.

Sending your recording

Keep Melon connected. This takes a few seconds.

Done

Melon restarted with your recording. Unplug the USB cable, switch your case back on and press the button.

If your computer says the drive was not ejected properly, you can ignore it. Melon disconnects itself after the update.
